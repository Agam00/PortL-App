Privacy Policy — Portl

Last updated: 28 July 2026

Portl (“we”, “us”, “the app”) is a society/community management app for residents, security guards, and society administrators. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your rights. Portl is provided to residents through their housing society; accounts are created by invitation from a society administrator.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your full name, email address, phone number, role (resident, guard, or admin), and your assigned flat/tower/society. Passwords are stored only as a secure salted hash.
  • Visitor management data: visitor names, phone numbers, visit times, and — when a guard captures one — a visitor photo, used to log entry/exit at the gate.
  • Messages & community content: chat messages you send to other members, and posts/comments you publish in the community feed.
  • Service & society activity: amenity bookings, service requests, complaints/helpdesk tickets, dues/payment records, poll responses, and vehicles you register.
  • Notifications: a device push token (via Apple Push Notification service / Firebase Cloud Messaging) so we can deliver alerts and messages.
  • Basic technical data: device platform (iOS/Android) associated with your push token.

How we use your information

  • To operate the core service: gate/visitor management, pre-approvals, messaging, notices, amenities, dues, and community features.
  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • To send you relevant notifications (visitor approvals, alerts, messages, notices).

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

Sharing

Your information is visible to other members of your own society as needed for the service (e.g., a guard sees a resident’s name/flat to verify a visitor; residents see the society directory). We share data only with infrastructure providers that operate the service on our behalf: our hosting/database provider and the push-notification services (Apple APNs, Google/Firebase FCM). We may disclose information if required by law.

Data retention & deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from within the app (Profile → Delete Account). Deleting your account removes your personal profile data, credentials, chat messages association, and push tokens, and signs you out on all devices.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS/TLS. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and access tokens are short-lived. Access to society data is restricted to authenticated members of that society.

Children

Portl is intended for adult residents and society staff and is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

You may access or update your profile in the app, delete your account, or contact us to request removal of specific data.
